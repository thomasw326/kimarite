## User approval round 1

Approved display names: **Denshamichi — Runaway Charge**; **Gaburi — Belly-Driving Attack**; **Ottsuke — Forearm Clamp**; **Power Water — Chikara-mizu Ritual**. **Ankogata** remains unchanged as an internal and provisional display name until its original meaning is confirmed. Historical questions above are retained for audit; these approvals supersede their former proposals.

## User approval round 2 and semantic corrections

Approved: **Driving Force — Power Surge**, **Belt Slap** (no subtitle), **Reset to Neutral — Break Contact**, **Sidestep — Turn Aside**, **Tottari — Arm-Bar Throw**, **Utchari — Last-Ditch Throw**. The user clarified **Belt Slap** means the pre-bout pump-up ritual (not a literal slap/strike); UI rules text should not portray it as a strike. The user clarified **Ankogata** means **big-belly sumo**, superseding the inaccurate speculative 'Anchored Stance'. Its exact player-facing subtitle remains open to review; do not treat 'big-belly sumo' as an official kimarite translation.

## User approval round 2 and design clarification

Approved: Driving Force — Power Surge; Belt Slap (no subtitle); Reset to Neutral — Break Contact; Sidestep — Turn Aside; Tottari — Arm-Bar Throw; Utchari — Last-Ditch Throw.

**Meaning corrections:** Belt Slap describes the **pre-bout pump-up ritual**, not a physical belt strike; the old proposed “Disrupting Strike” was inaccurate. **Ankogata** means **big-belly sumo** in this project, not an anchored stance; “Ankogata — Big-Belly Sumo” is a proposed literal label pending final wording approval. These clarifications supersede the earlier open questions in the draft.

## User approval round 3

Approved: **Ankogata — Big-Belly Sumo**; **Attempt Deep Outside Grip** (retain exact name); **Attempt Deep Inside Grip** (retain exact name); **Maesabaki — Hand-Fighting Control**; **Deashi — Advancing Footwork**; **Tsukiotoshi — Thrust-Down**. These supersede any earlier REVIEW notes for those names. The historical questions above remain for traceability.

## User approval round 4

Approved: **Shiko — Ritual Stomp**, **Kannuki — Double-Arm Lock**, **Kirikaeshi — Twisting Knee Trip**, **Fumikomi — Explosive Step**; preserve the original names **Gain Left Grip** and **Gain Right Grip** without subtitles. These decisions supersede the earlier REVIEW questions.

The generated **Power Water Cantrip** is displayed as **Power Water Refresh** (no Japanese subtitle). **Crushing Pressure — Overwhelming Force** is approved per the earlier naming direction.

# B12 ranked AI difficulty (NPC-only)

**Status:** implemented in `b12_difficulty.py`, applied by `b12_ai_day.py` only during the 20 AI-versus-AI bouts resolved after each player's bout.

## The pyramid

| Banzuke ranks | Tier | Base difficulty |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | 1 | 8 |
| 2–3 | 2 | 7 |
| 4–6 | 3 | 6 |
| 7–10 | 4 | 5 |
| 11–15 | 5 | 4 |
| 16–21 | 6 | 3 |
| 22–28 | 7 | 2 |
| 29–36 | 8 | 1 |
| 37–42 | 9 | 0 |

Each of the 60 AI identities also has a **hidden permanent −2, −1, 0, +1 or +2** modifier generated by SHA-256 of `(world_seed, wrestler_id)`. This is deliberately not displayed on the banzuke, opponent preview or AI audit response. Its value never changes during the six tournaments, including when reserve AIs get promoted. **Current difficulty is rank-band base + personal modifier**; it changes only when an AI's rank changes *between basho*, not after each bout.

A hidden variation can make a nearby lower-ranked wrestler harder than a higher-ranked one. The rank-based baseline still decreases exactly one step at each tier.

## What difficulty changes in NPC-vs-NPC matches

1. **Card composition:** start from the assigned, unique 28-card B12 framework; protect the starting Kimarite and the **12 specifically approved duplicate/reward originals**. For every two positive difficulty levels (rounded up; max five swaps), replace a lower-style-affinity **non-reward, non-Kimarite** card with a strictly higher-style-affinity eligible card. At negative difficulty do the same in reverse. Affinity uses the current V5 six-axis style weights; it does not use a random rarity score. Cards are chosen deterministically; never alter the 28 physical-original count or 12 designated duplicate rewards. A card is swapped only when its affinity strictly improves/weakens the deck.
2. **Stats:** apply the existing optional V5 **seven-step stat boost table** (primary / secondary / tertiary priorities), now enabled specifically for ranked **NPC-vs-NPC** matchups. At difficulty 7 this adds +1 to all three starting stats; at difficulty 8 it also boosts tertiary. Negative difficulty never penalizes base stats; it weakens card selection only.
3. **Earned duplicates:** a wrestler still gets precisely one of its 12 source-designated duplicate cards per **previous win in the current basho**, capped at 12. No extra card rewards are added by rank; physical deck sizes remain 28–40.
4. **Winner:** normal Tachiai and Exchange rules, current `style_ai_v5`, card effects and authentic Kimarite/ring-out conditions are always used. Rank or difficulty is never an automatic winner, win-probability modifier or simulated coin flip.

## Player-versus-AI bouts: unchanged

The player-matchup challenge was already based on ongoing **rank/record-based opponent selection** and the opponent's live W–L earned duplicates. That path still calls `build_ai_fighter` with **no** difficulty override and receives the exact framework's existing 28+reward cards and existing base six-point style stats, regardless of banzuke rank.

This separation is intentional: the pyramid shapes how the AI field's results and rankings evolve while preserving the currently playable human difficulty curve and its prior testing. Classic/practice bouts, the player's 2/1/0 stats, draft, Tachiai concealment, victory rewards, card locks and rank-transition formula are unchanged.

## Save stability and backward compatibility

Difficulty is calculated from existing saved `seed`, stable `wrestler_id` and current `rank`. This adds **no new required schema fields** and does not change existing save/restore behavior. Already finished matchdays retain their recorded real outcomes; only as-yet-unplayed AI-versus-AI bouts use the new pyramid.

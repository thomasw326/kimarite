Five-round tournament · 15 bouts

Choose a deck and play five rounds of three bouts. Each round deals nine cards to divide into three openings. All 15 random opponents are unique, and each round’s three opponents are revealed before you assign cards.

Assign the nine cards

Each group needs three distinct dealt cards, with no mandatory Kimarite in player-controlled mode. Classic mode retains its previous roles. Cards cannot be reused.

WELCOME TO KIMARITE

Your first sumo year

Build your own wrestler, then compete in up to six tournaments of 15 bouts each. Your choices in the stable shape what happens in the ring.

  1. In the Heya (stable): Name your wrestler and assign simple Power, Technique and Spirit stats. Start with eight cards and draft 20 more, choosing one from three each time.
  2. Prepare three bouts: Inspect your cards and opponents, then allocate nine opening cards: three for each bout.
  3. Enter the Dohyō (ring): Commit zero to three opening cards to Tachiai. Kept cards become your starting Exchange hand. Your opponent's opening stays hidden until you commit.
  4. Fight and return: Play cards, manage Push, Position and Momentum, and win by a Kimarite or ring-out. After the bout, return to the Heya for your next decisions.
  5. Build your legacy: Winning bouts can earn duplicate card choices (up to 12 per tournament). Those selected cards become the automatic starters of your next tournament's fresh 28-card draft.
Key terms and combat resources
Heya
Your stable: drafting, preparation, rewards and tournament results.
Dohyō
The clay sumo ring, where you resolve Tachiai and fight.
Tachiai
The initial clash. Committed cards contribute their orbs and opening effects.
Exchange
The turn-by-turn card play after the opening clash.
Kimarite
A winning technique: meet its conditions to claim the bout.
Push / Position
Push shifts the shared Position track toward an edge. Position +4 or −4 is still inside the ring; crossing beyond it causes a ring-out.
Momentum
A separate advantage track; various cards and Kimarite use it.
Grip
Left- and right-hand belt control; cards may improve it or disrupt it.
Temporary
A card effect that remains active for a limited time or until consumed.
